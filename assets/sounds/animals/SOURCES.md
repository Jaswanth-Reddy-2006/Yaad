## 🔒 License Compliance Statement
- **Zero Proprietary Recordings**: No copyrighted music, movies, or proprietary sound libraries were used.
- **Zero Human Speech Fallback**: Sounds are 100% genuine animal acoustic audio waveforms, preserving natural listening experiences for elder cognitive engagement.
- **Offline Reliability**: Audio is bundled locally in `assets/sounds/animals/` and encoded as instant Data URIs in `constants/AnimalAudioData.ts` to operate completely offline.
